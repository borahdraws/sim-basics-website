# "I have no idea what I'm doing"

Hi! My name is Borah (she/her) and I'm an FRC alum and mentor.&#x20;

I am an artist and painter who loves learning new things. Coding can be fun even for people who are completely non-technical like me.

I will be the first to admit I need a little more help than others, but there aren't many FRC programming tutorials. Many of the tutorials are in video format which are especially difficult for me to follow.

I wanted a single resource for me to keep track of what I've learned, and I thought that there may be others who might find my thought process helpful as well.

***

I use Windows 11 Java, AdvantageKit/Advantagescope and PhotonVision for FRC robot code.

I hope this resource will encourage anyone interested to start researching independently because much of programming is looking up and learning things on your own!
